## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene synthesis of organic botanical vitality and handcrafted architectural timber aesthetics. Drawing deeply from Nordic modernism and Japanese *Wabi-Sabi* sensibilities, the digital experience mirrors a sunlit living space finished in untreated white oak, brushed linen, and potted greenery.

### Personality & Audience
- **Target Audience:** Homeowners, interior design enthusiasts, architects, and lifestyle-conscious users seeking calm, intentional living spaces and tactile, thoughtful home customization.
- **Brand Personality:** Organic, quiet, grounded, architectural, and restorative. It completely rejects high-energy digital tropes, neon saturation, tech-futurist gradients, and stark clinical neutrals.
- **Emotional Response:** An instant sense of breathability, domestic warmth, tactile stability, and natural clarity. The interface acts as a silent canvas that allows natural materials, space, and home layouts to take center stage.

## Colors

The palette is strictly anchored in natural elements—earth, timber, and botanical flora. It operates exclusively in a light, sun-washed mode. Pure pitch blacks, harsh digital grays, and synthetic neon values are prohibited.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Deep Chestnut Brown (`#6B4F3A` / Deep: `#5C4433`):** Represents weathered timber and aged leather. Utilized for brand identity, primary interactive triggers, and high-emphasis focal elements.
- **Secondary — Leaf / Botanical Green (`#4A7C59` / Light Tint: `#E8F0E5`):** Expresses new foliage and indoor vitality. Used for positive affirmation, dynamic badges, environmental toggles, and live states.
- **Tertiary — Muted Brass / Warm Wood (`#C9A961`):** Evokes mid-century brass hardware and polished light maple. Dedicated to metrics highlights, decorative badges, ratings, and structural accents.
- **Base Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - **Main Canvas:** Soft linen eggshell (`#FAF8F3`).
  - **Sidebar / Structural Panels:** Warm architectural linen (`#F5F2EC`).
  - **Component Cards & Floating Layers:** Clean crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with an ultra-soft sand boundary (`#EDE8E0`).
- **Typography & Text Neutrality:**
  - **High-contrast Primary Text:** Deep warm espresso (`#2C2418`)—never `#000000`.
  - **Secondary / Metadata Text:** Warm earthy gray (`#7A6E5E`).
  - **Dividers & Structural Borders:** Gentle warm linen grout (`#EFEAE2`).

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects a dialogue between an intellectual editorial warmth and clean architectural precision.

- **Editorial Headings (`Newsreader`):** Infuses titles and primary showcase statements with literary dignity, timeless craftsmanship, and the quiet pace of printed lifestyle periodicals.
- **Interface & Body (`Manrope`):** Delivers geometric clarity and structural balance for long-form reading, technical dimensions, navigational chrome, and metadata tags without sacrificing human warmth.
- **Rhythm & Line Height:** Line heights are deliberately relaxed (1.5x–1.6x for body prose) to generate negative space that invites unhurried contemplation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured fluid grid inspired by architectural blueprinted floorplans and residential room proportions.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous outer margins (`2.5rem` to `4rem`). Sidebar panels remain fixed or dock cleanly to the architectural canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 2rem canvas padding. Floating panels fold into drawer overlays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) screen margins. Bottom-docked sheets replace lateral sidebars.

### Spatial Rhythm
Generous macro-spacing (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) isolates distinct home-staging sections, while micro-spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`) tightly clusters companion information (such as botanical tags, timber specifications, or dimensions).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses architectural layering, tonal surface stacking, and ambient botanical shadows rather than heavy synthetic drop-shadows.

### Shadow Character
All shadows are tinted with the primary deep chestnut spectrum rather than neutral charcoal or cold black:
- **Base Surface (`Level 0`):** `#FFFFFF` with 1px hairline border in `#EDE8E0`. No shadow.
- **Elevated Card / Module (`Level 1`):** `0 4px 20px rgba(107, 79, 58, 0.04)` combined with 1px border in `#EDE8E0`.
- **Floating Controls / Modals (`Level 2`):** `0 12px 32px rgba(107, 79, 58, 0.08)`, 1px border in `#EDE8E0`.

### Tonal Stratification
Depth is primarily established through background shifts:
- Background base: `#FAF8F3` (Eggshell Canvas)
- Integrated navigation and side rails: `#F5F2EC` (Linen Surface)
- Active cards and floating controls: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Cotton Canvas)

## Shapes

Shapes emulate organic woodwork edges, sanded curves, and smooth river pebbles:
- **Cards, Canvas Panels, & Modals:** Standardized on `1rem` to `1.5rem` (16px–24px) radius (`rounded-2xl` equivalent) for an inviting, tactile softness.
- **Inputs & Interactive Controls:** `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` (8px–12px) radius for precise hand ergonomics.
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** Full organic capsule pill radius (`9999px`) to mirror botanical seed and leaf geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Deep Chestnut (`#6B4F3A`), text in linen white (`#FAF8F3`). Hover state shifts to deeper bark (`#5C4433`) with subtle lift.
- **Secondary (Botanical):** Background in soft Leaf Green (`#4A7C59`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Used for positive and environmental actions.
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1px solid `#EDE8E0` border, background transparent, text in `#2C2418`. Hover state fills with warm linen tint (`#F5F2EC`).

### Sidebar & Navigation
- **Container:** Warm linen base (`#F5F2EC`) separated with `#EFEAE2` right border.
- **Active Navigation Item:** Pale Sage Green container (`#E8F0E5`), text in Deep Chestnut (`#6B4F3A`), anchored with a vertical Leaf Green (`#4A7C59`) indicator bar on the inner edge.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, standard 16px corner radius, hairline `#EDE8E0` border, and warm timber diffused shadow (`rgba(107, 79, 58, 0.04)`).
- Headers use `Newsreader` font pairing with subtle wood-grain icon badges.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#EDE8E0` border, 10px rounded corners. Focused state shifts border to Warm Chestnut (`#6B4F3A`) with a soft glow ring (`rgba(107, 79, 58, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked state in `#EDE8E0` frame; checked state filled in `#4A7C59` (Leaf Green) with crisp white tick/dot.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Natural Living / Verified Badge:** `#E8F0E5` background with `#4A7C59` leaf green text.
- **Material Accent Chip:** Linen neutral `#F5F2EC` background with `#7A6E5E` earthy text.
- **Highlight / Price Chip:** `#FAF4E6` background with `#C9A961` muted brass text.
